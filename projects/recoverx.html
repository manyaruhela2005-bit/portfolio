Project Overview

RecoverX is an optimization-based delivery recovery system designed to intelligently handle failed or disrupted deliveries. Developed by team "Little Coders," the project integrates Full-Stack development, AI/ML, and Optimization to convert a delivery disruption into a practical recovery recommendation.

The Problem

When a delivery fails (due to customer/rider unavailability, vehicle breakdown, or traffic), manually deciding what to do next leads to:

  • Increased delivery delays & additional travel distance
  • Higher recovery costs
  • Poor rider utilization
  • Missed time windows
  • Inefficient reassignment

The Solution

RecoverX solves this by automatically analyzing the current situation and generating optimized recovery plans. The system evaluates strategies such as:

  • Reassigning delivery to another rider
  • Rescheduling delivery
  • Rerouting the delivery
  • Selecting alternative feasible recovery options based on constraints and impact

Key Highlights

AI-Assisted Recovery

Uses machine learning models to support recovery decision-making by predicting classification and numerical impact of different choices.

Constraint-Aware Planning

Strictly checks rider availability, capacity, delivery time windows, and feasibility constraints before generating any recovery option.

Optimization with OR-Tools

Leverages Google OR-Tools to evaluate delivery recovery plans under constraints, ensuring the recommendation is the most practically sound choice.

Traffic-Aware Routing

Uses Haversine distance alongside dynamic traffic multipliers to estimate a realistic travel and delay impact.

Explainable Recommendations

The system explicitly explains why a particular recovery option was recommended, rather than simply presenting a black-box prediction.

Real-Time API Architecture

Uses a FastAPI REST backend to process recovery events and update relevant rider, order, and dashboard information dynamically.

Layer Roles

  • Frontend: Provides the delivery monitoring interface and centralized Recovery Centre for review.
  • Backend: Handles API requests, validation, business logic, and recovery engine communication.
  • ML Layer: Uses Random Forest models to support recovery-related predictions.
  • Optimization Layer: Evaluates feasible recovery plans under strict delivery constraints.
  • Recommendation Layer: Combines feasibility and recovery impact to produce an actionable recommendation.

AI & Optimization Engine

RecoverX integrates predictive machine learning with deterministic optimization algorithms.

Machine Learning Component

RecoverX relies on scikit-learn's Random Forest models for data-driven decision support.

  • Random Forest Classifier: Used for classification-related recovery decisions and determining option viability based on historical patterns.
  • Random Forest Regressor: Used for numerical predictions related to recovery impact and estimated delays.

The ML component acts as a supportive layer to guide the optimization process toward the most historically successful recovery strategies.

Google OR-Tools Optimization

OR-Tools is utilized to evaluate delivery recovery options deterministically while adhering to physical constraints.

The optimization engine accounts for:

  • Delivery delay and additional distance
  • Rider availability and vehicle capacity
  • Strict time windows and feasibility metrics

The goal is to find a practical, mathematically verified recovery plan rather than just making an isolated prediction.

Testing & Reliability

System stability is paramount when handling active deliveries. Our testing approach includes:

  • Pytest Framework: Automated testing pipeline.
  • 20 Unit Tests: Ensuring individual functions process constraints correctly.
  • 3 Integration Scenarios: Full end-to-end testing of the disruption-to-recovery workflow.
  • Rule-Based Fallback: A critical failsafe mechanism ensuring the system can still produce a viable recovery decision when the ML or optimization paths are unavailable or fail to converge.

Challenges

  • Handling multiple recovery constraints simultaneously without failing.
  • Algorithmically choosing between several closely competing recovery actions.
  • Effectively combining ML predictions with deterministic optimization.
  • Accurately incorporating dynamic distance and traffic impacts.
